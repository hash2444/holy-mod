Holy Mod started as 23 open-source community add-ons - ores and tools, bosses, origins and classes, storage tech, travel, building, furniture, lighting and creatures - merged into one behavior pack and one resource pack. On top sits **#2444's** own content and an integration layer that turns it into one game: one guide, one command set, a Chronicle of milestones, daily quests, world events and class bonuses that reach into every part.

> The add-ons' own content belongs to their authors (see [Credits & licenses](#credits--licenses)). The integration layer, the compatibility fixes, the merge into a single pack and everything under the `holy:` name are made by **#2444**.

## Features

### ✨ The Holy layer - one game instead of a pile of add-ons

- **One mod** - one behavior pack and one resource pack, one creative inventory with *Holy* groups, every command under `/holy:`, one `[Holy]` chat style, its own pack icon and welcome screen
- **Holy Guide** - a book with a chapter for every part of the mod, your profile and the **Command Center**, which runs every command from menus. Every player gets it on first join; craft a new one from a book and a gold ingot
- **Recipe Book** - every crafting recipe made by #2444 (941) by category, with the grid, the ingredients and a green/red check of your inventory. The **Cookbook** does the same for the oven and the kitchen machines
- **Holy Chronicle** - 146 milestones through every part of the mod, each with a reward - often something from another part
- **Daily quests** - three new quests every Minecraft day, drawn from 37 quest types all over the mod, plus a daily bonus
- **World events** about every 20 minutes (operators can set 5-60), for everyone at once: Gem Rush, Lucky Night, Boss Hunt, Builder's Blessing, Traveler's Winds, Quest Frenzy, Monster Bounty, Butterfly Bloom, Sweet Tooth Hour, Capsule Fever and Treasure Hunt
- **Your class counts everywhere** - Miners find extra rubies and gems and shrug off the Lucky Ore curse, Merchants get extra emeralds from Lucky Ore, Blacksmiths wear out ruby, gem and area tools slower, Lumberjacks get Haste from the lumber axe, Warriors and Archers hit bosses harder, Clerics heal everyone nearby when a boss falls, Explorers travel and drive faster, Beastmasters and Ranchers heal seals nearby, Cooks get more out of boss fruit
- **Your origin counts too** - Vampires drain life from bosses, Children of Zeus call lightning on them, Voidwalkers hit the Void Blossom harder, Elves shoot harder, Diviners sense nearby bosses, Inchlings earn XP for placing Holy decor, Felines rest by cat statues, Piglins find gold in Lucky Ore
- **Parts that feed each other** - bosses and Lucky Ore drop loot from the other parts, every diamond recipe also works with a ruby, a ruby path to the area tools, seal blubber burns in furnaces and makes candles, sitting on a chair heals (a lantern nearby cures fatigue), vehicles get headlights from a lantern
- **Leaderboard, ranks and profile** - milestones, bosses, quests and rubies per player; a rank under every name (Novice ... Holy Master); your profile shows origin, class, progress and where you died last
- **For operators** - switch every integration on or off in the guide, see `/holy:status`, start events with `/holy:event`
- **RTX ready** - every Holy block texture and model has a realistic material map: cloth, wood and stone stay matte, glass, water and cut gems are smooth, only gold, silver and steel are metallic, and only things that give off light in the game glow. Players without RTX see the normal look and can join the same world

## Install

Download **`Holy-Mod-v1.73.2.mcaddon`** from the [releases page](../../releases) (or straight from this repository) and open it - Minecraft imports the packs.

1. Create or edit a world and open **Add-Ons**.
2. Activate the **Behavior Pack** and the **Resource Pack** of this add-on.
3. Requires Minecraft Bedrock **1.26.40 or newer**.

If items are missing in your world, check the world's *Experiments* page and enable *Beta APIs* and *Holiday Creator Features* as a fallback.

New to add-ons? **[SETUP-HELP.md](SETUP-HELP.md)** walks you through it step by step.

## First steps

1. Open the **Holy Guide** - every player gets it on first join. It has a chapter for every part of the mod.
2. All commands start with `/holy:` - or run them from the **Command Center** in the guide without typing.
3. Check the **Chronicle** and your **daily quests** in the guide for ideas what to do next.
4. Start a **new world** (or explore new land) to find the new ores, clusters, flowers and creatures - world generation only appears in new chunks.

## What was changed to make this pack work

Every add-on was updated to run on current Minecraft (1.26.x): beta-only Script API calls were swapped for the closest stable equivalent, and several add-on bugs that only show up in the real game were fixed at build time - for example OriginsPE's player model (a script block Minecraft 1.26.52 no longer accepts, which crashed the game) and nine animals OriginsPE shipped as outdated copies. Two generation bugs were fixed: rubies (their spawn chance was written as a fraction instead of a percent, so almost none generated) and the Lucky Ore (a vein size of 1 places nothing in Bedrock). The area mining tools now drop rubies, gems and Lucky Ore loot. A handful of other candidate add-ons (furnideco, Prehistoric Rift, Vanilla Upgrade, Canopy, Low Price Villager, Universal Camo, Pseudo-Stations, an old Backpack add-on, Storage Utilities, a ComputerCraft port) were tried and left out because they needed real rewrites to run.

## Dev Book

Every pack of mine carries a small easter egg: craft the **Dev Book** with **9 logs** (any wood type, 3x3 in a crafting table) and right-click it. It opens like a book: page 1 the credits, page 2 what this mod is, page 3 the GitHub links (Minecraft cannot open links, so they are shown as text). It also sits in the creative inventory under *Equipment*.
